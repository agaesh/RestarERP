# RestarERP

### Restaurant Enterprise Resource Planning System

**One Platform. Multiple Companies. Multiple Brands. Complete Business Visibility.**



## About RestarERP

RestarERP is a restaurant-focused Enterprise Resource Planning (ERP) system designed to centralise and streamline business operations across multiple companies, restaurant brands and physical outlets.

Managing a restaurant business involves more than processing customer orders. It requires coordination across product management, purchasing, inventory, costing, pricing, sales and financial operations.

As businesses expand into multiple outlets and franchise operations, managing these processes independently becomes increasingly complex.

RestarERP aims to address these challenges by providing an integrated platform that connects restaurant operations with business management, enabling organisations to manage their operations efficiently and gain better visibility into their business performance.

## Business Scope

RestarERP is designed to support different levels of restaurant business operations:

- **Brand Owners (Franchisors):** Brand oversight, franchise relationships, royalty reporting and network-wide business visibility.
- **Franchise Operators (Franchisees):** Multi-company and multi-outlet management, purchasing, inventory, costing, pricing and financial operations.
- **Outlet Managers and Staff:** Point of Sale (POS), order processing, stock management and daily restaurant operations.

## Core Capabilities

- **Business Structure Management** – Manage companies, brands, franchise operations and outlets.
- **Product Management** – Manage products, raw materials, recipes, BOM and units of measurement.
- **Purchasing Management** – Manage suppliers, purchase orders and goods receiving.
- **Inventory Management** – Track stock movements, inventory levels and consumption.
- **Costing & Pricing** – Manage product costs, recipe costing and selling prices.
- **Point of Sale (POS)** – Handle restaurant sales, orders and payment transactions.
- **Finance & Accounting** – Manage financial operations, accounts payable, receivable and reporting.
- **Business Intelligence** – Provide operational insights and performance visibility across outlets and companies.

## System Architecture

RestarERP follows a distributed system architecture, with services organised around individual business capabilities and responsibilities.

The architecture aims to establish clear domain boundaries, independent business logic and service-level data ownership, allowing individual services to evolve independently.
